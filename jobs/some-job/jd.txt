Observability Engineer — Job Description (Grafana Suite + OpenTelemetry)  
Role purpose  
Build and run a modern observability platform onprem and in cloud that helps engineering teams detect 
issues early, troubleshoot quickly, and improve reliability. You’ll standardise telemetry (metrics, logs, 
traces) using OpenTelemetry and deliver actionable dashboards, alerts, and investigations using the 
Grafana suite.  
Key responsibilities  
Platform engineering (Grafana stack)  
In conjunction with architect - design, deploy, and operate Grafana components (as applicable): Grafana, 
Loki, Tempo, Mimir, Alerting, Synthetic Monitoring.  
Build and maintain multi-tenant observability patterns (folders, datasources, RBAC, naming conventions, 
retention).  
Create reusable dashboards, alert rules, and SLO views aligned to service ownership.  
Migrate and improve existing dashboards , with drilldown.  
Instrumentation & telemetry standards (OpenTelemetry)  
Define and roll out OpenTelemetry standards for:  
Tracing (context propagation, sampling strategies)  
Metrics (naming, labels, cardinality controls)  
Logs (structured logging, correlation IDs, trace-log linking)  
Provide and maintain OTel collectors (agent/gateway), pipelines, processors, exporters, and secure 
transport.  
Define details of auto-instrumentation where appropriate and support manual instrumentation for 
critical paths.  
Reliability & operations  
Partner with SRE/engineering teams to implement:  - SLIs/SLOs, error budgets, and alert tuning (reduce noise, improve signal)  - Incident response improvements (runbooks, post-incident actions, “golden signals”)  
Perform capacity planning and cost optimisation (retention, sampling, label cardinality, storage tiers).  
Security, governance, and compliance  
Implement access controls, auditability, and data handling controls for telemetry.  
Ensure platform resilience (HA, backups, DR) and safe change management.  
Enablement & collaboration  
Create onboarding guides, templates, and “paved road” tooling for teams.  
Coach engineers on observability best practice and troubleshooting workflows.  
Required skills & experience  
Strong experience with Grafana with Loki, Tempo, Mimir.  
Hands-on OpenTelemetry experience (Collector and agent configuration, SDKs, context propagation).  
Solid understanding of distributed systems and observability concepts   
Infrastructure skills: Kubernetes/Helm and Linux with S3 and networking basics.  
Query proficiency: PromQL, LogQL, TraceQL (or equivalent).  
Practical incident management and alerting experience.  
Nice-to-have  
Familiarity with cloud-managed observability services and hybrid connectivity patterns.  
FinOps-style telemetry cost management.  
Experience integrating AI/ML into observability solutions
