Who are we?
At Finastra, we’re a global leader in financial services software, dedicated to expanding access to financial services and shaping what’s next for the industry. Our technology powers mission‑critical solutions across Lending, Payments and Universal Banking, supporting over 7,000 customers, including 80% of the world’s top 50 banks, in more than 110 countries. 

About the Role
Corporate Lending Technology is seeking an experienced and strategic Director, Head of DevOps, to lead the modernization of our engineering platform across flagship products. This is a high-impact leadership role responsible for defining and executing the Target Operating Platform roadmap, building and scaling a centralized DevOps organization, establishing SRE practices, and delivering self-service CI/CD capabilities and standardized client deployment patterns across the portfolio.

The Director, Head of DevOps, will own the DevOps function end-to-end, building the team, tooling, and culture necessary to accelerate delivery velocity, improve operational reliability, and position Corporate Lending Technology on a modern, cloud-native, cloud-agnostic foundation.

Key Responsibilities: DevOps Strategy & Target Operating Platform

Define and own the DevOps strategy and Target Operating Platform roadmap across all Lending Products
Drive the organization's strategic shift to containerization, cloud-native, and cloud-agnostic architectures as the standard engineering baseline
Partner with engineering leadership, architecture, and executive stakeholders to align the DevOps roadmap with business and technology strategy
Champion a platform product mindset, treating internal engineering teams as customers, and continuously improving developer experience
Centralized DevOps Organization & Self-Service CI/CD

Build, lead, and scale a centralized shared DevOps team responsible for delivering self-service CI/CD frameworks to all product engineering teams
Define and enforce standardized CI/CD pipelines, toolchains, and governance practices across all Corporate Lending products
Develop reusable pipeline templates, infrastructure modules, and deployment blueprints that enable product teams to operate with speed and consistency
Reduce outer-loop friction and drive automation across build, test, release, and deployment workflows
SRE & Operational Excellence

Build and mature Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices from the ground up across the product portfolio
Establish Service Level Objectives (SLOs), error budgets, observability standards, and incident response frameworks
Drive a culture of operational excellence, proactive monitoring, and continuous reliability improvement
Balance delivery velocity with system stability and client uptime commitments
Client Deployment Standards

Design and publish standardized deployment patterns for clients, ensuring consistent, repeatable, and well-documented onboarding and release processes
Reduce client-facing delivery risk and variability through structured deployment frameworks and validated reference architectures
Collaborate with client-facing and product teams to align deployment standards with client expectations and contractual obligations
Team Leadership & Organizational Development

Recruit, mentor, and develop a high-performing centralized DevOps engineering team
Serve as a hands-on technical authority and trusted advisor for product teams navigating the cloud-native modernization journey
Establish communities of practice and engineering guilds to drive knowledge sharing and standards adoption across product lines
Own the roadmap for extending DevOps standards and platform practices into Retail Lending

15–20 years of overall software engineering and technical leadership experience, with a strong and consistent focus on DevOps, platform engineering, infrastructure modernization, and cloud-native delivery
