## 3. KEY TALKING POINTS

### On the B2B eCommerce Domain Gap (likely first question):
> "My 12+ years at Nokia were spent building and running platforms that serve global enterprise customers across 30+ countries — high availability, SLA-driven, customer-facing. The platform engineering patterns are the same whether the workload is telecom or eCommerce: Kubernetes, CI/CD, monitoring, incident management, vendor SLAs, budget governance. I've run the full stack — infrastructure through observability — at a scale most eCommerce platforms don't reach."

### On Platform Consolidation (BD merger is top of mind):
> "I led a major platform consolidation at Nokia — migrated 100s of workloads across 50+ clusters to a unified platform while modernising 8-9 products. That involved working across multiple teams, handling legacy systems, managing risk during cutover, and keeping 99.95% availability throughout. Post-merger platform consolidation is the same playbook — assess, plan, migrate, validate, cut over."

### On People Leadership:
> "I built and led a 15-engineer team across 5 global locations. Set up a DevOps Center of Excellence, ran training programs on cloud-native and SRE practices, and coached engineers through career growth. I've assessed skill gaps, built targeted training plans, and grown the team from 8 to 15. I provide regular feedback and create development plans — not just annual reviews."

### On 24x7 Operations:
> "I've run 24x7 on-call for production platforms for years — not just participating, but designing the rotation, the escalation paths, the runbooks. When a P1 hits, I lead the war room. I set up incident management and RCA processes from scratch at Nokia, which cut operational incidents by 40% and MTTR by 35%."

### On CI/CD & Build/Release Maturity:
> "I built CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins, GitLab CI, and ArgoCD across 8+ product teams. Cut deployment cycles by 50% with 100% reproducible deployments. The key was self-service — teams could provision and deploy without waiting on platform engineers. I also set up environment management standards and config-as-code practices so releases are consistent and auditable."

### On FinOps & Budget Management:
> "I launched a FinOps program from zero at Nokia — introduced rightsizing, tagging, reserved instances, and automated cost policies. Cut cloud spend 15% YoY and prevented $500K in overspend. I've managed vendor relationships, tracked SLAs, and handled budget forecasting for infrastructure spend."

### On Cloud-Agnostic Skills (if Azure comes up):
> "The JD mentions AWS, Azure, or GCP — my depth is in AWS. But Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and FinOps are cloud-portable. I'm CKA-certified, so moving between EKS and AKS is a configuration difference, not a capability gap. I'd be productive on Azure within weeks."
