## 2. Financial Health

Revenue: $2.96B (2024). S&P 500 company with strong operating margins (~28%). Profitable every year.

**BD Merger (huge deal)**: In July 2025, Waters agreed to merge with Becton Dickinson's Biosciences & Diagnostic Solutions business — $17.5 billion deal. Completed February 2026. This roughly doubles the company's scope and brings in diagnostics and biosciences capabilities. There are shareholder lawsuits ($17.5B) alleging gaps in the proxy statement. Something to keep an eye on.

**Wyatt Technology acquisition** (May 2023): Bought for $1.36B cash. Light scattering instruments.

**Growth signals**: Expansion mode — the BD merger, 55 open positions on AmbitionBox in India, and hiring a Senior Manager for Digital Platform Engineering all point to investment in tech infrastructure. The merger likely created new platform consolidation work.

**Market position**: Dominant in liquid chromatography. Held 40% market share in LC in the 1970s and still a top-2 player globally.

## 6. Strategic Interview Insights

**What problem are they solving with this hire?**

They're building or restructuring Digital Platform Engineering in Bangalore. The role owns the cloud platform, DevOps pipeline, and digital ecosystem — likely B2B eCommerce and customer-facing digital products. The BD merger (completed Feb 2026) probably increased infrastructure scale and complexity. This hire needs to consolidate, stabilize, and modernize.

**Buzzwords to mirror**:
- "Deliver Benefit" — Waters' founding motto. Drop it naturally.
- Reliability, availability, scalability — the JD is SRE-heavy
- "High-volume B2B eCommerce" — frame your Nokia experience as serving global enterprise customers
- Agile, Kanban — they want process maturity
- Mentorship, talent development — the people management section is unusually detailed in the JD

**Red flags to probe**:
- BD merger closed 3 months ago. Ask about integration status — are you inheriting two platforms? How much tech debt?
- JD mentions both "Digital Platform Engineering" and "Digital Platform Operations" — one team or two? What's the reporting structure?
- "24x7 technical support with on-call" — is this a rotation or are you the single escalation point?
- Promotions rated 3.1 on AmbitionBox — ask about career paths for Senior Managers
- Gender ratings gap (4.3 men vs 3.1 women) — worth understanding team dynamics

**Questions to ask**:
1. "How has the BD merger affected the digital platform? Are you consolidating two separate stacks?"
2. "What's the current cloud footprint — primarily AWS, Azure, or multi-cloud?"
3. "How large is the team today, and is there approved headcount to grow it?"
4. "What's the biggest platform reliability problem right now?"
5. "Who does this role report to — someone in India or the US?"
6. "What does the vendor landscape look like — managed services or mostly self-hosted?"
