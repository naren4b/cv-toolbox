# LinkedIn Profile Optimization — Candidate

> **Purpose**: Actionable guide to upgrade every LinkedIn section for recruiter visibility and inbound leads.
> **Tone**: Professional but natural — sounds like you wrote it yourself, not like a branding agency did.

## WRITING RULES — SOUND HUMAN, NOT AI

- Write LinkedIn sections the way a real engineer would. Not a motivational speaker.
- **BANNED phrases**: "passionate about", "thought leader", "proven track record", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "holistic", "results-driven professional", "seasoned", "dynamic", "leveraging"
- Don't stuff every bullet with buzzwords. One or two keywords per line is enough.
- Use "I" naturally. Don't write in third person ("the candidate is a...").
- About section should read like you explaining what you do to a smart friend, not a press release.
- Short sentences. No walls of text. Break things up.
- If a sentence sounds like something a LinkedIn influencer would post, rewrite it.

### Why It Matters
Recommendations are **social proof** that can't be faked. Profiles with 3+ recommendations get significantly more recruiter views.

### Tips
- **Give first**: Write recommendations for 3–4 people before asking. Most reciprocate.

- **Timing**: Ask right after a successful project or award (you have 4 awards in 2025 — perfect timing).

## 10. LINKEDIN SEARCH ALGORITHM TIPS

| Factor | What LinkedIn Weighs | Your Action |
|---|---|---|
| Headline keywords | Heaviest weight in recruiter search | Use exact terms: Kubernetes, AWS, Terraform, EKS |
| Skills (endorsed) | Second-heaviest | Reorder + get endorsements on top 5 |
| About section keywords | Indexed for search | Include all target role keywords naturally |
| Activity / posting | Boosts profile visibility in feed | Post 1–2x/week (even reshares with commentary) |
| Connection count | 500+ shows as "500+" which is sufficient | Focus on quality over quantity |
| Profile completeness | "All-Star" status gets more views | Complete every section |
| Engagement on posts | Signals authority to the algorithm | Reply to comments on your posts within 1 hour |
