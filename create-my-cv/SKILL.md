## HOW TO TAILOR A CV (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1 — Analyse the JD
1. Extract the **job title**, **company**, and **seniority level**.
2. Identify **must-have** skills (hard requirements).
3. Identify **nice-to-have** skills separately.
4. Note keywords repeated multiple times — these are high-priority.
5. Identify the **domain** (telecom, fintech, SaaS, cloud-native, etc.).
6. Note any specific **tools**, **certifications**, or **years of experience** called out.

### Step 2 — Match & Prioritise from This Profile
1. For each must-have skill, find matching entries from **Section A (Skills by Technology Domain)** and **Section C (Quantified Achievements)**.
2. For each nice-to-have, do the same but mark as lower priority.
3. From **Section B (Skills by Role Type)**, select the role archetype closest to the JD and pull relevant bullets.
4. If the JD emphasises leadership, pull from the **Leadership & People Management** sections.

### Step 3 — Compose the Tailored CV
1. **Headline**: Rewrite using the JD's job title and the first 4–5 keywords from the JD's requirements section.
2. **Summary**: 3–4 sentences blending the candidate's experience with JD language. Mirror their terminology.
3. **Core Competencies**: Reorder to lead with skills the JD emphasises. Drop irrelevant categories.
4. **Professional Experience**: Reorder bullet points so the most JD-relevant achievements appear first. Trim bullets that don't map to any JD requirement.
5. **Metrics**: Always include quantified impact (%, $, scale numbers) — these are in Section C.

### Step 4 — Final Checks
- [ ] Every must-have keyword from the JD appears at least once in the CV.
- [ ] Summary mirrors the JD's language and tone.
- [ ] Bullet points are ordered by relevance to the JD, not chronology.
- [ ] CV length is 2 pages maximum.
- [ ] No skills or achievements are fabricated — only content from this SKILL.md.
- [ ] Every sentence sounds like a human wrote it — not a bot, not a template engine.

### Writing Rules — Sound Human, Not AI

These rules apply to every CV, summary, and bullet point generated from this profile:

- Write like a senior engineer updating their resume on a Sunday, not like an AI prompt output
- Use short, direct sentences. Vary length. Fragments are fine.
- **BANNED words/phrases** (instant AI giveaway): "leverage", "utilize", "spearheaded", "orchestrated", "endeavour", "in order to", "it's worth noting", "I bring a wealth of", "I'm passionate about", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "holistic", "delve into", "proven track record"
- **BANNED bullet starters**: "Successfully...", "Responsible for..."
- Start bullets with plain, strong verbs: Built, Ran, Led, Cut, Shipped, Set up, Moved, Migrated, Designed, Fixed, Rolled out, Wrote
- Prefer plain English always:
  - "set up" not "established"
  - "ran" not "managed"
  - "built" not "architected"
  - "cut" not "reduced"
  - "moved" not "migrated" (unless migration is the actual term)
  - "rolled out" not "implemented"
  - "runs on" not "is underpinned by"
- No exclamation marks in CVs
- Don't stack adjectives: "highly scalable, resilient, cloud-native, production-grade platform" — pick two max
- Read every sentence aloud. If it sounds like a LinkedIn post or press release, rewrite it.
