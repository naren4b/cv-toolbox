# Recruiter Reply Skill

## Purpose

Draft concise, factual recruiter replies and cover letters from the candidate information in Google Drive.

## Workflow

1. Read the recruiter email and job description from the Google Drive company folder.
2. Use approved candidate facts from Google Drive `data/`.
3. Draft the reply or cover letter in that same company folder.
4. Mark the draft as ready for user review; never send it.

## Rules

- Do not include private compensation, notice-period, or contact details unless the user directs it.
- Do not invent availability, compensation, eligibility, or experience claims.
- Keep all candidate-specific information in Google Drive.
